Version: 0.21.0
Date: 2026-10-14
  Features:
    - Crew cards: a card for each of your crew in the bottom right of the screen, kept current: what they're doing
      (or saying), health, armor, gun, ammo and fish, how they go about, and their record (kills, built, trips,
      times down). A ring in their colour while they talk, red while they fight; grey with a countdown while
      they're down. Idle, a card sinks to a strip and rises under the mouse. Click one for their pockets: take a
      stack, or all they carry. With the fse loader they're a web page in your fse-std theme; without it (or in
      multiplayer) the game's own GUI.
    - New setting "Crew cards" (on by default; also in the window's gear panel) to hide them.

Version: 0.20.0

Version: 0.18.1
Date: 2026-10-12
  Changes:
    - Every text of the window, its settings and the Fire question can be translated (locale); the settings'
      checkboxes are worded as the mod settings they set.
    - Buttons that shared a name tell apart: the everyone row's "Clear" is "Deconstruct", the goal's is "Drop goal";
      "Come" and the member's "Follow" are both "Follow me"; everyone's "Stop" is "Stop all".
    - The "New crew" setting shows Roam, Follow me, Stay (not roam, follow, stay).
    - The dock below the window is as wide as the window and follows its resizing (one row of orders when they fit,
      else two); a crew member's status takes the room left and shows all of it in its tooltip.
    - "Everyone:" as wide as its words, both rows of orders lined up after it; stat captions capitalised.
    - A third number on the Crew page, how many are busy; the three tiles share the row. "AI chat is off – set a key
      in" the gear, when it doesn't answer.
    - The inputs say what they are: "Name" by Hire, "Item" and "×" before the count, "Have" on the Goal page, "Say"
      by the chat box. "Production line" only shows with bpgen (it looked switched off without it); its rate only
      takes input while it's ticked.
    - The settings are no taller than the window (they hid the dock's orders) and all their folds are as wide.
    - "Docked", "Theme..." and "Reset size and place" are fse-std's own words, as in the other mods' windows; Fire's
      question has a red Fire.
    - A crew member's line over their head is cut short with "…" (the whole of it is in chat) and sits above a nearby
      teammate's line instead of across it.
  Bugfixes:
    - The crew button stayed lit after closing the window with its X.
    - By the screen's left edge, the window's page tabs (out of its left side) went off the screen: the window is kept
      where they show, also after a move or a resolution change.
    - Inside the window, the Fire buttons touched the scroll bar.
    - With GUI Unifyer, the crew button lost its lit look when a player joined with the window open.
